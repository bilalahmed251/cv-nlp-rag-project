# CONSTRUCTION SITE SAFETY POLICY & COMPLIANCE MANUAL

## SECTION 1: Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
1.1 Helmet/Hard Hat Policy: All personnel, visitors, and contractors entering the active construction zone MUST wear an approved safety helmet (hard hat) at all times. No exceptions are granted.
1.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance: Failure to wear a safety helmet will result in an immediate fine of $50 for the first offense, and removal from the job site for repeated violations.
1.3 High-Visibility Vests: High-visibility reflective vests must be worn in areas with operating heavy machinery or vehicles.
1.4 Safety Eyewear & Footwear: Steel-toed boots and protective eye goggles are required when welding, drilling, or cutting materials.

## SECTION 2: Hazard Communication & Risk Protocol
2.1 Risk Levels:
- LOW RISK: Minor non-compliance, such as unbuttoned vest or temporary removal of goggles in designated safe rest zones.
- MEDIUM RISK: Working near low-hazard machinery without proper eye or foot protection.
- HIGH RISK / CRITICAL: Operating at heights without a harness, or entering active construction zones without a hard hat (helmet).

## SECTION 3: Emergency Reporting
3.1 Immediate Reporting: Any observed safety violation or unmitigated hazard must be reported immediately to the Site Safety Officer.
